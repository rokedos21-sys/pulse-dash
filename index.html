Hold Space, ↑ or click/tap to go up and release to go down. In wave mode you fly at 45° and can only slide along walls at their angle. In ship mode you thrust up while holding and fall when you let go; land on blocks, but side hits crash you. In UFO mode tap to hop (you can hop again in mid-air). In ball mode tap to flip gravity between floor and ceiling. Mix levels switch modes at white portals. Esc opens the menu, M toggles sound. Turn on practice mode with P in the menu, then press Z to drop a checkpoint and X to remove it. Pink portals shrink the wave; orange and blue ones change the speed; purple ones flip gravity, so holding dives instead of climbing. The Endless Portal in the menu never stops and gets harder the further you go.
